Installing MagicEQ on a Mac
===========================

1. Copy the plugins into your plugin folders (in Finder press Cmd+Shift+G and paste the path):
     MagicEQ.component  ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/     (Logic, GarageBand, Ableton, etc.)
     MagicEQ.vst3       ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/           (Ableton, FL Studio, Cubase, Reaper, etc.)

2. These test builds are not notarized by Apple yet, so macOS may block them. Open Terminal and run:
     xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/MagicEQ.component
     xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/MagicEQ.vst3

3. Restart your DAW (Logic: it scans new Audio Units on launch). MagicEQ appears under "RainWorks".

MagicEQ.app is a standalone version you can open without a DAW (run the same xattr command on it first).
